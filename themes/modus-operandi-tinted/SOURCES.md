ANSI slots use the upstream terminal sequence: black, red, green, yellow, blue, magenta, cyan, white, followed by their bright variants. Fixed terminal black and white remain upstream values, so applications explicitly printing white on pale paper can produce low contrast. The ordinary text, muted text and semantic accents use legible defaults. Contrast claims concern opaque role/background pairs, not every arbitrary ANSI pair or every wallpaper pixel.

## Original wallpapers

Generated on 2026-11-12 using OpenAI's built-in **image_gen** tool, one fresh generation per asset, without reference images. All four are native **1672 × 941 PNGs**, copied unchanged without resizing, cropping, recoloring or upscaling. Each was visually inspected for composition, material detail, unwanted text, and quiet desktop space. The generated images are distributed under the repository's GPL-3.0-or-later terms to the extent rights apply. No third-party photographs are included.

The warm paper, limestone portico, olive walk and sculptor's table offer a small visual ode to Prot's thoughtful craft and Mediterranean surroundings. They are imagined places, not depictions of his home or property.
